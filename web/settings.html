Instinct · TypeSafe Jev, optional

Optional reflexes. Jev is a small, fast model that never speaks; it answers yes/no questions about the conversation in about a third of a second, so she starts a motion as she promises it and her face reacts while you are still talking. Without a key, or when Jev is slow or unsure, the built-in rules decide.

While on, the text of the current exchange goes to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai) for each decision; audio never does. Typical cost is a few cents per hour of conversation.
